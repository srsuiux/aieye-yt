# Contributing

Thanks for helping. AI Eye is an MIT-licensed framework for people who run it themselves with their own keys.

- **Keep it generic.** Nothing about a specific channel, person or account belongs in the repository. Channel details live in
  git-ignored files under `data/`.
- **No secrets or media.** Never commit keys, tokens, `.env`, `data/`, or generated pictures, audio and video.
- **Spend nothing in tests.** Do not add tests or examples that call paid APIs without an explicit confirmation step.
- **Write plain language** in the interface and docs: say what a button does and what it costs.
- **Open an issue first** for large changes, so we agree on the direction.

By contributing you agree your work is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Only contribute work you have the right to share.
