Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

AI Eye publishes short educational videos on YouTube and Facebook. This policy explains how we handle information.

What we collect

This website collects nothing. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or ads.

When you watch, like, comment on or follow our videos on YouTube or Facebook, those platforms collect information under their own policies. We only see what they show every page owner, such as view counts, comments and aggregate audience statistics. We do not receive your personal data.

Comments and messages

If you comment on or message our pages, we may read and reply to you. We do not sell, share or use that content for anything else, and we do not build profiles of viewers.

Children

Our videos are made for a general, family audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not post personal details such as full names, addresses or phone numbers in comments.

Third-party services

We use AI services to help write and produce videos, and YouTube and Facebook to publish them. Viewer data is not sent to the AI services.

Data deletion

To have a comment or message of yours removed from our pages, contact us through our YouTube channel or Facebook page and we will delete it. Anything stored by YouTube or Facebook can be managed in your account settings with them.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the date above.
