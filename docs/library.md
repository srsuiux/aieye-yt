# Library and uploads

Open **Library** from the left menu, or go to `/library`.

## Waiting to upload

Videos that were made but not uploaded stay on your computer until you act. That includes videos made with upload set to
**none**, videos you skipped at review, and videos whose upload failed. Each one has:

- a player,
- an editable **title**, and **description and tags** under a fold,
- **who can watch it**, set to private by default,
- **Upload to YouTube** and **Delete video**.

Uploading opens a run page that shows the upload progress. When it finishes, the video's folder is removed from your computer and
the YouTube link is recorded in your history.

!!! warning
    Choosing **public** publishes the video as soon as the upload finishes. The page asks you to confirm.

**Delete video** removes the file from your computer for good. The page asks first.

The upload button is disabled while another run is in progress, or when YouTube is not connected. Connect it on the
[Connections page](/docs/connections).

!!! note
    The 3-videos-a-day limit counts videos pushed to YouTube, including uploads from here.
